# Parliament

## Simple Explanation
Parliament is the Union legislature of India and includes the President, Lok Sabha, and Rajya Sabha. It makes laws, approves finances, and keeps the executive accountable through questions, debates, and motions.

## Key Points
- Parliament consists of the President and two Houses: Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.
- Lok Sabha is directly elected and controls the fate of the Council of Ministers through collective responsibility.
- Rajya Sabha is a permanent House and cannot be dissolved.
- Money Bills can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha.
- UPSC often asks about powers, composition, sessions, and the differences between the two Houses.

## Exam Relevance
This is a high-frequency Polity topic because it links institutional design with actual legislative procedure. Questions often cover composition, special powers, money bills, and executive accountability.
