# Preamble

## Simple Explanation
The Preamble is the introductory statement of the Constitution. It declares the source of constitutional authority, the nature of the Indian state, and the broad ideals the Constitution seeks to achieve.

## Key Points
- It begins with "We, the People of India," showing popular sovereignty.
- It describes India as sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic, and republican.
- It highlights justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity as core constitutional ideals.
- The Preamble helps interpret the spirit and purpose of constitutional provisions.
- UPSC often asks whether the Preamble is enforceable and how it relates to constitutional philosophy.

## Exam Relevance
This topic is important because it connects constitutional theory with interpretation. UPSC uses it for conceptual questions on constitutional values, amendments, and landmark cases such as the Kesavananda Bharati judgment.

## Practice Questions
- Why is the Preamble described as the key to the Constitution?
- Which constitutional ideals are explicitly mentioned in the Preamble?
- Is the Preamble enforceable in court?
- How did the 42nd Amendment affect the Preamble?
- Why is the Preamble useful in mains answers on constitutional values?
