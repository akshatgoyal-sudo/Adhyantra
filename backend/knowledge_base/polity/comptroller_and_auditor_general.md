# Comptroller and Auditor General

## Simple Explanation
The Comptroller and Auditor General of India, or CAG, is the constitutional authority that audits government accounts. The office promotes financial accountability and parliamentary control over public expenditure.

## Key Points
- The CAG audits the receipts and expenditure of the Union and the states.
- The office is an important institution for public financial accountability.
- Audit reports help legislatures examine whether public money was used properly.
- Independence of the CAG is crucial for credible financial oversight.
- UPSC often asks why the CAG is called the guardian of the public purse.

## Exam Relevance
This topic is important because it links Parliament, public finance, and accountability. Questions often focus on audit functions, independence, legislative committees, and the limits of the CAG's role.

## Practice Questions
- Why is the CAG described as the guardian of the public purse?
- How does the CAG support parliamentary oversight?
- What is the difference between auditing expenditure and controlling policy?
- Why is independence important for the office of the CAG?
- How do audit reports become relevant in exam questions on accountability?
