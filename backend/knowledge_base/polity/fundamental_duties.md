# Fundamental Duties

## Simple Explanation
Fundamental Duties are moral and civic obligations expected from citizens. They were added to emphasize that rights and constitutional values must also be supported by responsible conduct.

## Key Points
- Fundamental Duties were added by the 42nd Amendment.
- They are listed in Article 51A.
- They are non-justiciable but constitutionally significant.
- They encourage respect for the Constitution, national symbols, public property, and civic harmony.
- UPSC often asks about their origin, nature, and relation to rights and values.

## Exam Relevance
This topic is useful because it links constitutional morality with citizenship and public responsibility. Questions usually focus on amendment history, enforceability, and value-based governance.

## Practice Questions
- Why were Fundamental Duties added to the Constitution?
- Are Fundamental Duties enforceable in court?
- Where are Fundamental Duties located in the Constitution?
- How do duties complement rights?
- Why does UPSC ask duties alongside constitutional values?
