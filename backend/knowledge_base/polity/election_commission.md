# Election Commission

## Simple Explanation
The Election Commission of India is the constitutional body responsible for conducting free and fair elections. It supervises elections to Parliament, state legislatures, and the offices of President and Vice President.

## Key Points
- The Election Commission is an independent constitutional authority.
- It conducts and supervises elections to major constitutional offices and legislatures.
- It prepares electoral rolls and oversees the election process.
- Independence of the Commission is important for democratic legitimacy.
- UPSC often asks about the powers, composition, and constitutional status of the Election Commission.

## Exam Relevance
This topic is important because it connects democracy, representation, and electoral integrity. Questions often test constitutional provisions, independence, model code issues, and the distinction between constitutional and statutory election bodies.

## Practice Questions
- Why is the Election Commission considered vital to free and fair elections?
- Which elections fall under the supervision of the Election Commission?
- How does constitutional status support the independence of the Election Commission?
- Why does UPSC ask about electoral rolls and election administration?
- What is the difference between the Election Commission and a State Election Commission?
