# Parliament Sessions

## Simple Explanation
Parliamentary sessions are the periods during which Parliament meets to conduct legislative, financial, and accountability business. They are important for understanding how constitutional institutions function in practice.

## Key Points
- Parliament usually meets in Budget, Monsoon, and Winter sessions by convention.
- Sessions are summoned by the President on the advice of the government.
- Question Hour, debates, and motions make sessions central to executive accountability.
- The Constitution requires that not more than six months pass between two sessions.
- UPSC often asks about session-related procedures and their practical significance.

## Exam Relevance
This topic helps bridge static Polity with actual legislative functioning. It is especially useful in exam questions on parliamentary oversight, legislative calendar, and accountability tools.
