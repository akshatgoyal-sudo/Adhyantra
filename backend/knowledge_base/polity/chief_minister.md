# Chief Minister

## Simple Explanation
The Chief Minister is the real executive head of the state government. The office leads the Council of Ministers, shapes policy, and acts as the main link between the Governor and the elected government.

## Key Points
- The Chief Minister is usually the leader of the majority in the legislative assembly.
- The Chief Minister heads the state Council of Ministers.
- The Governor appoints the Chief Minister, but the choice is guided by legislative majority.
- The Chief Minister communicates decisions of the Council of Ministers to the Governor.
- UPSC often tests the relationship between the Chief Minister, Governor, and state legislature.

## Exam Relevance
This topic is important because it explains how parliamentary government operates in the states. Questions often compare the Chief Minister with the Prime Minister and test appointment, powers, and collective responsibility.

## Practice Questions
- Why is the Chief Minister called the real executive at the state level?
- How is the Chief Minister appointed?
- What is the Chief Minister's role in relation to the Governor?
- How is the Chief Minister similar to the Prime Minister?
- Why does legislative majority matter for the office of Chief Minister?
