# Soil Types in India

## Simple Explanation
India has varied soil types because of differences in rock structure, climate, relief, vegetation, and time. Soil types are studied not only as physical geography topics but also through their relation to agriculture and land use.

## Key Points
- Alluvial soil is widespread in northern plains and supports intensive agriculture.
- Black soil is associated with lava regions and is known for its moisture-retaining capacity.
- Red, laterite, desert, mountain, and saline soils each have distinct regional patterns and limitations.
- Soil fertility depends on texture, mineral content, organic matter, and moisture conditions.
- Soil degradation through erosion, salinity, and nutrient depletion is a major policy concern.

## Exam Relevance
UPSC often asks about distribution, characteristics, crop suitability, and degradation of Indian soils. It also connects soils with monsoon, agriculture, and conservation practices.
