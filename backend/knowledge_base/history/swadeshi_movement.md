# Swadeshi Movement

## Simple Explanation
The Swadeshi Movement emerged in response to the partition of Bengal in 1905 and promoted boycott of foreign goods along with support for indigenous industry and national education. It marked a shift from limited petitions to wider political mobilisation.

## Key Points
- The movement began as a protest against the partition of Bengal but quickly gained an all-India political meaning.
- Boycott, swadeshi, national education, and passive resistance became key methods of mobilisation.
- It encouraged indigenous enterprise and linked economic self-reliance with nationalism.
- The movement helped radicalise Indian politics and strengthened the extremist strand within the national movement.
- Its social base remained uneven, but its political influence was far wider than Bengal alone.

## Exam Relevance
UPSC often asks about the causes, methods, ideological significance, and limits of the Swadeshi Movement. It is important for understanding the transition from moderate politics to mass-oriented nationalist mobilisation.
