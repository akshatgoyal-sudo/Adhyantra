# Indian National Congress

## Simple Explanation
The Indian National Congress was founded in 1885 and gradually became the main political platform of the Indian national movement. It evolved from a moderate petitioning body into the central organisation of mass anti-colonial struggle.

## Key Points
- The Congress began with moderate constitutional demands and political dialogue with the colonial government.
- Over time, nationalist politics expanded through extremist, Gandhian, socialist, and mass-movement phases.
- Congress sessions became important spaces for political resolutions, leadership shifts, and ideological debates.
- The organisation played a central role in linking local grievances with an all-India national movement.
- Its history is often studied through phases such as moderates, extremists, Gandhian mass movements, and late-colonial negotiations.

## Exam Relevance
UPSC often asks about the foundation of the Congress, the moderate-extremist divide, session-based milestones, and the organisation's role in transforming elite politics into a mass national movement.
