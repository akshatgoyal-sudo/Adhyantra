# Prime Minister

## Simple Explanation
The Prime Minister is the real executive head in the Indian parliamentary system. The office coordinates the Council of Ministers, leads the government in Parliament, and shapes national policy direction.

## Key Points
- The Prime Minister is appointed by the President but must command Lok Sabha confidence.
- The Council of Ministers works under the Prime Minister's leadership.
- Cabinet formation, portfolio allocation, and policy coordination depend heavily on the Prime Minister.
- The Prime Minister links the President, Parliament, Cabinet, and administration.
- UPSC often contrasts the Prime Minister's real authority with the President's formal position.

## Exam Relevance
This topic is frequently asked because it reveals how parliamentary government actually functions. Prelims and mains both use it for questions on collective responsibility, cabinet government, and executive leadership.

## Practice Questions
- Why is the Prime Minister called the real executive?
- How is the Prime Minister linked to Lok Sabha confidence?
- What is the Prime Minister's role in cabinet formation?
- How does the Prime Minister connect the President and Council of Ministers?
- Why does UPSC compare the President and Prime Minister?
