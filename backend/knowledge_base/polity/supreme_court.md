# Supreme Court

## Simple Explanation
The Supreme Court is the highest judicial authority in India. It protects the Constitution, interprets laws, settles disputes, and safeguards Fundamental Rights through judicial review and constitutional remedies.

## Key Points
- The Supreme Court is the apex court in the Indian judicial system.
- It has original, appellate, advisory, and writ jurisdiction.
- Article 32 allows direct access to the Supreme Court for enforcement of Fundamental Rights.
- Judicial review allows the Court to examine the constitutional validity of laws and executive actions.
- UPSC often asks about jurisdiction, judicial review, and the Court's role in the basic structure doctrine.

## Exam Relevance
This topic is central to polity because it connects rights, federal disputes, constitutional interpretation, and landmark cases. Questions often test the Court's jurisdiction and the difference between Supreme Court and High Court powers.

## Practice Questions
- Why is Article 32 called the heart and soul of the Constitution?
- What is the difference between original and appellate jurisdiction of the Supreme Court?
- How does judicial review strengthen constitutionalism?
- Why is the Supreme Court important in federal disputes?
- How does the Supreme Court contribute to the basic structure doctrine?
