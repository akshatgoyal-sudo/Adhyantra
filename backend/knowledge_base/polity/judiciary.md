# Judiciary

## Simple Explanation
The judiciary interprets the Constitution, settles disputes, protects rights, and upholds the rule of law. It is designed to remain independent so that constitutional limits on power remain meaningful.

## Key Points
- The Supreme Court stands at the top of the integrated judicial system.
- Judicial review allows courts to examine the constitutional validity of laws and actions.
- Judicial independence is protected through tenure, salaries, and institutional safeguards.
- High Courts and subordinate courts form the broader judicial structure.
- UPSC often asks about judicial review, judicial independence, and court jurisdiction.

## Exam Relevance
This topic is central to Polity because it links rights, federal disputes, constitutional interpretation, and separation of powers. It is especially important in questions on landmark doctrines and court powers.
