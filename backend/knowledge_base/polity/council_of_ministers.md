# Council of Ministers

## Simple Explanation
The Council of Ministers is the body that aids and advises the President and actually runs the Union government. It includes the Prime Minister and different categories of ministers responsible for policy and administration.

## Key Points
- The Prime Minister heads the Council of Ministers.
- The Council of Ministers aids and advises the President under the Constitution.
- Cabinet Ministers form the core decision-making group within the larger council.
- The Council is collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha.
- UPSC often tests the difference between the Cabinet and the wider Council of Ministers.

## Exam Relevance
This topic matters because it explains how parliamentary government works in practice. Questions often focus on collective responsibility, ministerial hierarchy, cabinet role, and the relationship between the President and the Council.

## Practice Questions

- Why is collective responsibility central to parliamentary government?
