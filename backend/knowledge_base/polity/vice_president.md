# Vice President

## Simple Explanation
The Vice President is the second-highest constitutional office in India. The office is important mainly because the Vice President serves as the ex officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha and may act as President in certain situations.

## Key Points
- The Vice President is elected indirectly by members of both Houses of Parliament.
- The office is not part of the Union executive in the same way as the President and Council of Ministers.
- The Vice President acts as the Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
- The Vice President may discharge the functions of the President during vacancy, absence, or inability.
- UPSC often asks about the difference between the election and functions of the President and Vice President.

## Exam Relevance
This topic is important because it links Parliament, constitutional offices, and succession arrangements. UPSC frequently tests the election method, removal process, and the Vice President's role in the Rajya Sabha.

## Practice Questions
- How is the Vice President elected, and how is this different from the election of the President?
- Why is the Vice President called the ex officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha?
- In what situations does the Vice President act as President?
- How can the Vice President be removed?
- Why does UPSC compare the Vice President with the Speaker of the Lok Sabha?
