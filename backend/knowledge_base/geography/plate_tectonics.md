# Plate Tectonics

## Simple Explanation
Plate Tectonics explains that the Earth's lithosphere is divided into moving plates that interact at their boundaries. This theory helps explain earthquakes, volcanoes, mountain building, and continental movement.

## Key Points
- Lithospheric plates move over the semi-fluid asthenosphere due to internal Earth processes.
- Convergent, divergent, and transform boundaries produce different landforms and geophysical events.
- Plate interactions explain the formation of fold mountains, ocean trenches, and mid-ocean ridges.
- The theory unified earlier ideas such as continental drift and sea-floor spreading.
- It is important for understanding the tectonic setting of India, including the Himalayas and earthquakes.

## Exam Relevance
UPSC frequently asks about plate boundaries, related landforms, earthquake and volcano belts, and the tectonic basis of the Himalayas. It also uses plate tectonics in conceptual physical geography questions.
