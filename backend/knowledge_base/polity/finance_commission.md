# Finance Commission

## Simple Explanation
The Finance Commission is a constitutional body that recommends how financial resources should be shared between the Union and the states. It helps maintain fiscal balance in the federal system.

## Key Points
- The Finance Commission is constituted periodically by the President.
- It recommends the distribution of tax revenues between the Union and the states.
- It also suggests principles governing grants-in-aid to states.
- The Commission is important for cooperative fiscal federalism.
- UPSC often asks about vertical and horizontal devolution in relation to the Finance Commission.

## Exam Relevance
This topic is important because it connects federalism with public finance. Questions often focus on constitutional status, functions, grants, and comparison with bodies like the NITI Aayog or GST Council.

## Practice Questions
- Why is the Finance Commission important in Indian federalism?
- What is meant by vertical and horizontal devolution?
- How does the Finance Commission support fiscal balance?
- Why does UPSC compare the Finance Commission with other fiscal institutions?
- What is the role of grants-in-aid in Centre-state financial relations?
